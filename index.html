About

Technology is most useful when people can understand it.

My career has moved through communication, cybersecurity operations, incident response, cloud protection, and artificial intelligence. That path shaped the way I approach engineering: understand the system, understand the risk, and explain both clearly.

I’m especially interested in the point where cybersecurity and AI meet — how organizations can adopt powerful AI capabilities without losing sight of security, governance, trust, human judgment, or the data those systems depend on.

This site is both a professional portfolio and a long-term home for projects, talks, experiments, lessons learned, and pieces of family history worth preserving.
